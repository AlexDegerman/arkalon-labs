# Changelog

All notable changes to the platform will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - Facility Launch (23.11.2026)

### Added

- **20-Tier Equipment & Century Mutations:** Launched 20 progressive generator tiers spanning **Research Desk** through **Absolute Void Compressor**, organized across four operational classes (`Computation`, `Energy`, `Reality`, `Arkalon`), with 6 progressive SVG visual stages, bulk purchasing (`x1`, `x10`, `x100`, `MAX`, `MAX ALL`), tapered quantity milestones, and four Century Mutations (`M100`, `M250`, `M500`, `M1000`).
- **Synthesizers & Secondary Economy:** Added 4 dedicated Synthesizer buildings (**Dust Condenser**, **Sub-Space Dredger**, **Cosmic Telemetry Array**, **Entropic Crucible**) generating passive Artifact Dust (scaling by `+50%` per run Era) and Operation Points alongside Probe speed and Synthesizer amplification bonuses.
- **Generator Modules & Endgame Overdrive:** Added 3 upgrade tracks per generator (**Output Boost**, **Cost Discount**, **Cross-Boost**), **10 Cross-Generator Synergy Links**, a **2x Maxed Set** bonus at the core cap (`Lv5`, up to `Lv8` via `AC5`), progressive Overdrive caps up to **Lv250** (`Tier I–III` / `Era 3–7` / `O10`) with pure `BigInt` scaling, `All` / `Upgradable` filters with collapsible maxed rows, and a quoted **Upgrade All** button.
- **44-Node Research Matrix:** Implemented 40 standard research nodes across four branches (**Computation `C1–C10`**, **Energy `E1–E10`**, **Reality `R1–R10`**, **Arkalon `O1–O10`**) plus 4 repeatable Infinite nodes (`C_INF`, `E_INF`, `R_INF`, `O_INF`), Grid & Dependency Tree views, up to 3 parallel branch slots, Active Overclocking (`25%` time cut), Study Queue presets (`Shortest`, `Cheapest`, `Rush Tier`), and `O6` single-prerequisite bypasses.
- **Three Nested Prestige Tiers & Automation Suite:** Introduced **Tier I (Reality Recalibration / AR)**, **Tier II (Timeline Severance / CF)**, and **Tier III (Singular Synthesis / OS)** with 32 permanent upgrades (`16 AR`, `10 CF`, `6 OS`), `Resonance Memory` AR floor preservation, 12 toggleable automation systems, and **Smart Auto-Prestige Calibration** (`Fixed AR`, `Multiplier`, `PPS Stall`) with megaproject hold guards.
- **Relics & Class Resonance:** Added 20 unique Relics across five rarity tiers (`Common` through `Mythical`) and four classes, `3–6` active sockets, `60s` swap cooldowns (removable via CF *Relic Preservation*), `2-piece` (`+15%` effects) and `3-piece` (`+35%` effects + `+10%` class output) **Class Resonance**, 3 Loadout Presets (`Speedrun`, `Deep Idle`, `Surge Hunter`), and deterministic pity protection for Relics `4` and `9`.
- **Excavation & Sub-Space Probes:** Built the Probe Assembly Bay supporting up to 8 probes (`25K → 10¹⁴²` RP ladder) across **Safe Sectors** (5m), **Unstable Rifts** (20m), **Void Depths** (2h), **Singularity Rift** (8h), and **Omega Horizon** (24h), with active-to-daily expedition pacing, scaled repair cycles, re-priced Sub-Space Dredgers (50K base), modular subsystems (**Aegis Shielding**, **Tachyon Thrusters**, **Deep-Core Siphon**), Batch Deploy, Probe Recall, and 6 Veteran Ranks (**Rookie** → **Apex Drone**).
- **Spatial Anomalies & Particle Collider:** Added 4 Standard Anomalies, 4 Operation Anomalies, and 2 Era 5+ Fused Anomalies with a `10%` passive cascade chance and manual on-demand ignition via the **Particle Collider** (10 minutes of current Dust income, minimum `25`).
- **Megaprojects & Endless Mode:** Launched 3 milestone structures (**Chronos Array**, **Arkalon Matrix Mirror**, **Omega Singularity Sphere**) and the infinitely repeatable **Arkalon Dyson Swarm**, featuring `25% / 50% / 75% / 100%` phased construction perks, a `0–100%` RP allocation slider, Omega Convergence Victory at `10¹⁶²` RP + Node `O10`, and Endless Mode up to `10¹⁰⁰⁰`.
- **Anomalous Operations:** Introduced deterministic 90-day rotating cosmic epochs, the **Cosmic Retuning Device** (1 frequency override per run), 4 permanent Operation Artifacts, 2 repeatable Cosmic Sinks, and 4 Cosmic Titles.
- **24 Facility Challenges & Custom Mutator Sandbox:** Implemented 12 Standard (`SC1–SC12`), 8 Advanced (`AC1–AC8`), and 4 Extreme (`EC1–EC4`) multi-tier challenges with lossless pre-challenge run restoration, Readiness Benchmarks, carry-over summaries, best active time tracking, and a post-`EC4` **Custom Mutator Sandbox**.
- **33 Achievements, 7 Eras & 60 Visual Number Tiers:** Added 33 tracked milestones (`+1%` global RP each plus 5 milestone capstones), 7 Progression Eras, and 60 animated CSS tier styles scaling from `Thousand` through `Trequinquagintillion` (`10¹⁶²`) with `Suffix` and `Scientific` notation support.
- **Arkalon Overseer, Audio & Customization:** Built the 5-stage evolving wireframe Arkalon Core, typewriter Overseer terminal, Web Speech API Arkalon Voice synthesis, dynamic BGM crossfading, Web Audio polyphonic SFX, 5 interface themes, Neon Bloom / CRT Scanlines / Reduced Motion toggles, desktop Keyboard Command Matrix, and mobile PWA navigation.

### Technical Foundation

- **Cached-PPS `BigInt` Engine:** Decoupled `100ms` tick accumulation from multiplier recalculation so `recalculatePPS` runs only on state-changing inputs, supporting exact `BigInt` arithmetic up to `10¹⁰⁰⁰` with an endless softcap above `10³⁰⁸`.
- **Segmented Geometric Bulk Math:** Implemented exact `BigInt` logarithmic conversions (`bigIntLog10`) and boundary-aware `costToBuyN` / `maxAffordable` calculations across `R1` and `M100` cost-scaling transitions.
- **Stepped Offline Simulator:** Built a multi-segment offline engine (`8h / 12h / 24h`) that steps across research completions, auto-queued nodes, anomaly expirations, megaproject completions, probe cycles, and `EC3` forced collapses.
- **Arkalon Core SSO & Cloud Persistence:** Integrated cross-app identity provisioning with `network.rpsleague.fi`, 5-second `localStorage` autosaves, 60-second PostgreSQL cloud syncs with server-side sanity checks and 30-second rate limiting, `BroadcastChannel` heartbeat multi-tab protection, and zero-rerender DOM refs for 60fps RP animation.
